# Folio PC · Tibetan Practice

English | [中文](README_ZH.md)

Folio is an offline Windows workbook for learners using *拉萨口语读本（课堂教学）*. You can study Tibetan vocabulary, practise reading and sentence building, and return to words when they are due for review. The interface is available in English and 中文.

**[Download Folio for Windows](https://github.com/Tyanmood/folio-pc-releases/releases/latest/download/FolioSetup.exe)** · [Release notes](https://github.com/Tyanmood/folio-pc-releases/releases/latest)

## What’s in the workbook?

- **Lessons 1–7** have dedicated activities. Lesson 1 introduces Tibetan letters and sounds. Later lessons include dialogues, grammar explanations, translation and sentence building. **Lessons 8–17** will be released as we learn them.
- **Vocabulary study** lets you choose one lesson or combine several. Learn up to ten new words at a time in textbook order or mixed order. Choose a meaning for an easier round, or reveal a flashcard and judge your recall for a harder one.
- **Revise** brings words back at increasing intervals as you remember them. A missed word comes back sooner. You can also practise early, fill in Tibetan spelling, or type the Chinese meaning.
- **Listening and reading** lets you look up words inside a passage and mark phrase breaks as you read. You can enlarge just the Tibetan text or choose a black theme for the workbook.
- **Import a page** turns a photo or pasted list into a personal vocabulary deck. You can check and correct the entries before saving them.

## Get started on Windows

1. Run **FolioSetup.exe** while online. If setup asks whether you already use Folio, choose **No** for a new installation, or **Yes** to select your existing **Folio PC.html** and keep your progress.
2. Open **Folio PC.html** in your browser. For a new installation, you’ll find it in **Documents → Folio PC**. You can now practise offline.
3. Open **My workbook**, choose a lesson, then select **Learn new words**. Pick your order and difficulty and press **Start learning**. Next time, open **Revise** to see which words are due.

Folio saves answers, reading marks and recordings in your browser profile. Reopen the same file in the same browser profile to continue where you left off.

## Updates

Folio checks GitHub automatically when you open the workbook online. When **Download and install update** appears, click it to update your workbook in place. The current public edition is **Folio PC 1.1**. Its GitHub build tag is `v1.3.1`, which older copies may show in the update prompt.
